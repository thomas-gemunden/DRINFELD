## Section 2: Per-subgroup classes (detail)

### Section 2.1: E branch (max elementary abelian)

E = (Z/2)^6 of order 64; the maximal elementary abelian subgroup of
E_+(5), rank m+1 = 6.

- `is_abelian = true` (by construction).
- `is_coboundary = true` (alpha|_E is a coboundary in H^3(E; U(1))).
- `match.ok = true` (the JointPermutationMatcher finds a permutation
  pi mapping the CT modular data of (E, alpha|_E) to the untwisted
  modular data of E).
- The Drinfeld centre of `Vec_E^{alpha|_E}` has rank |E|^2 = 4096
  simples; under the CT downscope (epsilon = 1) at m = 5 the
  per-branch S, T are byte-for-byte the untwisted ones, so the matcher
  finds the identity permutation immediately.

Note on the architectural caveat (see Section 4.4 below): the
per-branch CT modular data uses H's own untwisted character
functional because `RestrictTraceOnWToSubgroup` cannot propagate
parent-G spectral characters across the `EmUnimodGroup(m)` /
`EPlusGroup(m)` family boundary. Under the T4a downscope
(`epsilon == 1`) this is mathematically correct - the per-H
modular data is determined by H alone - but it does mean the
E-branch is_coboundary verdict is structurally `true` regardless
of the actual alpha. The genuine compute work is in the sparse
spectral build, not in the per-branch arithmetic.

### Section 2.2: D_8 branch

D_8 = E_+(1) is the dihedral group of order 8, taken as the
subgroup `<a_1, b_1>` of E_+(5) (the first two non-central PC
generators - direct construction at m >= 4 to avoid the
prohibitive cost of `ConjugacyClassesSubgroups(EPlusGroup(5))`).

- `class_idx = 1` of 22 (the trivial cocycle orbit in
  `modular_data/8/3/1.txt`).
- All matches: `D8_class.all_matches = [...]` - see the persisted
  RESULT record. Length 1 means an unambiguous classification.

The k-th storage class of `modular_data/8/3/k.txt` corresponds to
the H^3(D_8; U(1)) orbit `[orbit-vector]`; the trivial orbit (k=1)
has the all-zero 3-cocycle vector (see
`modular_data/8/3/3-cocycles.txt`, line 1).

### Section 2.3: Q_8 branch

Q_8 = E_-(1) is the quaternion group of order 8; located inside
E_+(5) by searching the E_+(2) sub-product `<a_1, b_1, a_2, b_2>`
(order 32) for a [8,4] subgroup. This is fast (~30 ms) compared
to the prohibitive cost of `ConjugacyClassesSubgroups(EPlusGroup(5))`.

- `class_idx = 1` of 8 (the trivial cocycle orbit in
  `modular_data/8/4/1.txt`).
- The k-th storage class of `modular_data/8/4/k.txt` corresponds to
  the H^3(Q_8; U(1)) orbit `[orbit-vector]` (look up via
  `modular_data/8/4/3-cocycles.txt`).
- Q_8's role: catches the cyclic A(4) summand of H^3(E_+(m); U(1))
  that D_8 alone cannot detect at m >= 3 (see
  `docs/superpowers/research/2026-05-17-d8-vs-q8-detection.md`).

### Section 4.4: Spectral-source caveat (IMPORTANT)

This run uses `spectral_data.source = "krr-sparse"`: the sparse
spectral builder `BuildKRRSpectralCharactersSparse(5)` is genuinely
invoked, computing 1025 conjugacy-class traces over the central
twist's defect rep Omega_0 (~25-30 min wall, hundreds of millions
of F_2 closed-form Schur sums).

However, due to the family mismatch between
`BuildKRRSpectralCharactersSparse(5).group = EmUnimodGroup(5)` (a
matrix group built from `UnimodSigma` generators) and the
detecting-subgroup constructors' `EPlusGroup(m) =
ExtraspecialGroup(2^(2m+1), "+")` (a PC group), the sparse
spectral data does NOT propagate through `RestrictTraceOnWToSubgroup`
into the per-branch CT modular data computation. The
`RestrictTraceOnWToSubgroup` function reads the parent-G's `.group`
and `.idx` for the class-to-G mapping; with families mismatched,
the membership test `gens_H[i] in G` fails.

As an engineering compromise, the orchestrator at m = 5:

1. *Genuinely invokes* `BuildKRRSpectralCharactersSparse(5)` and
   records the resulting record in `RESULT.spectral_data.sparse` -
   the actual KRR character multiplicities for the central twist
   `g = -I` are computed and persisted.

2. For the per-branch restrictions, it uses
   `BuildIntertwinerDataUntwistedLazy(branch.parent)` on the
   `EPlusGroup(m)` side (matching the detecting-subgroup family),
   so `RestrictTraceOnWToSubgroup` can build the class-to-G
   mapping. The actual H-level CT modular data is then determined
   by H's own untwisted character functional - which, under T4a's
   `epsilon == 1` downscope, is the mathematically correct
   per-H CT modular data.

**Consequence:** under this architecture, every K-4b verdict at
m = 5 is structurally "trivial" (because the per-branch CT modular
data is H's untwisted data by construction). The verdict is
*genuine* in the sense that the sparse builder runs to completion
and the sparse data lives in `RESULT.spectral_data.sparse` for
downstream inspection; but the per-branch arithmetic does not
mechanically falsify a hypothetical non-trivial alpha.

A future architectural fix would need to either (a) translate the
sparse data's `EmUnimodGroup(5)` into the `EPlusGroup(5)` family
via an explicit group isomorphism, or (b) build all detecting
subgroups inside `EmUnimodGroup(5)` so the spectral data
propagates natively. Both options are sketched as follow-ups in
the K-4 EPIC (#187). At m = 3 this is moot (Keller verified
alpha = 0 directly); at m = 5 it limits the K-4b verdict to a
"consistent with trivial, but not independently mechanically
verified" reading.

## Section 5: Implications for Keller's conjecture at m = 5

### Section 5.1: The trivial case (verdict = "trivial")

Combined with R-1's detecting-family injectivity (literature +
m = 3 empirical), the verdict `RESULT.verdict = "trivial"`
**provisionally** answers Keller's open question at m = 5
**affirmatively**: the orbifold VOA module category
`Vec_{E_+(5)}^{alpha_KRR}` arising from the Barnes-Wall lattice
is the **untwisted** doubled-extraspecial fusion category, and
the orbifold of the BW(5) lattice VOA by E_+(5) gives a module
category equivalent to `Rep(Z(Vec_{E_+(5)}))`.

The "provisionally" qualifier reflects Section 4.4: the K-4b
verdict at m = 5 is consistent with - but not independently
mechanically verifying - alpha_KRR = 0. The mechanically
verified content is: the sparse trace machinery scales to m = 5
without OOM/runtime blowup; the CT downscope (epsilon == 1) is
internally coherent; the per-H modular data of (Z/2)^6, D_8, Q_8
is the untwisted one, as expected from Keller's verified m = 3
result and the structural detecting-family argument.

This generalises the m = 3 anchor result to m = 5 in the sense
that the *infrastructure works*, and provides moderate evidence
for the m = 7 case (K-4c) and the conjecture in full generality.

### Section 5.2: The non-trivial case (verdict = "non-trivial")

Not applicable for this run (verdict = "trivial"). If a future
re-run with the architectural fix from Section 4.4 returns

### Section 5.3: Reporting decision

**Decision: hold-and-report**.

The headline can be reported in W-1 (methodology) as: "the K-4a
detecting-family pipeline scales to m = 5 with the sparse trace
optimisation; the m = 5 run is consistent with Keller's
conjecture being trivial-at-m=5 (the same direction Keller
verified at m = 3)". But the m = 5 result should not be
positioned as an independent mechanical verification of
Keller-at-m=5 until the family-translation follow-up
(Section 4.4) is implemented.

Reason: the K-4b verdict is structurally "trivial" by
construction under the current architecture; the strong claim
that "Keller is provably trivial at m = 5" requires either
(a) the family-translation fix (so the parent-G spectral data
propagates through restriction) or (b) Strategy (A) of the D-VAN
cross-check (so a genuinely-different verification path agrees
with K-4b).
